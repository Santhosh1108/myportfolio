MetricLens — AI Product Analytics Reporting Agent

A cloud-first workflow that turns structured product data into KPI analysis, funnel diagnostics, segment insights, AI-assisted hypotheses, and automated decision-ready reports.

GitHub ↗

The problem

Product information can be spread across events, user attributes, feature usage, and historical metrics. Turning it into a repeatable product review can require manual analysis and interpretation.

Question: Can a lightweight AI workflow transform structured product analytics into a repeatable, decision-oriented report while keeping the underlying metrics deterministic?

AI layer

Gemini is not the source of truth for the KPI calculations. MetricLens computes numerical evidence first, then uses Gemini to interpret that evidence.

Evidence

Calculated KPIs, funnel movement, segments, feature adoption, and historical metrics.

Hypotheses

Potential explanations are clearly separated from observed evidence.

Experiment

The report translates product friction into a measurable experiment recommendation.

Guardrails

Primary and guardrail metrics keep recommendations measurable.

Limitations

The included portfolio dataset is synthetic. Pre/post comparisons are descriptive and do not establish causality. Gemini model availability is account/API dependent.
