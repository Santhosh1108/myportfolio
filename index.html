A bit about me

Curious first. Practical second.

I’m interested in products where the hard part is not writing the feature list, but understanding the person and the problem behind it.

I enjoy the uncomfortable middle: ambiguous problems, half-formed ideas, and figuring out what to build before anyone asks for it.

My work usually sits between product thinking and actually making the thing. That means research, flows, metrics, prototypes, APIs, automation, and plenty of iteration until the idea feels simple on the other side.
