[ SECTION 02 // OPERATIONAL COMPASS ]

The PM job is systematically reducing uncertainty.

Good product management isn't ritualistic ceremonies. It is creating high context, making intentional sacrifices, and letting feedback drive the steering wheel.

01 CONTEXT

Understand Before Prescribing

Who is actually bleeding? What are they clumsily doing right now to patch it? Why solve it today vs next quarter? No wireframes exist until friction is proven.

• Rule: If the user interview lacks emotion, you haven't found the problem yet.
02 DECISION

Make Trade-offs Visible

A roadmap is merely a published record of things you deliberately chose to kill. Document the anti-scope explicitly so engineering and sales don't assume it's hiding in sprint 3.

• Rule: Define what evidence would cause you to reverse this bet.
03 BUILD

Get Tactile in Hands Fast

Slide decks produce polite nods. Working software with mock data produces immediate visceral honesty. When users stumble over a button, all philosophical debate ends.

• Rule: The fastest prototype that tests the hypothesis always wins.
04 FEEDBACK

Let Evidence Call Shots

Gut feel is an acceptable spark for a hypothesis, but a terrible reason to stay on a dying path. If telemetry shows zero repeat usage, have the guts to kill the feature.

• Rule: Ship, instrument event hooks, read PostHog, pivot or double-down.
