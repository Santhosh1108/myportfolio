01 · Flagship · AI product development

SpecLens

An AI-assisted product development tool that takes a rough idea through discovery, structured specification, PRD generation, critique and an early MVP prototype.

  • Reframed the starting point from “write a PRD” to guided discovery, asking one focused question at a time so the user does not need all the answers upfront.
  • Created a structured product state covering problem, users, goals, requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria and open questions.
  • Used that shared state across PRD generation, critique, prototype generation and DOCX export so decisions carry through the workflow.
Product bet: the biggest bottleneck is often turning an incomplete idea into enough context for a team to review, challenge and build.

02 / How I think

The PM job, to me, is reducing uncertainty.

Good product work is not more process. It is better context, sharper decisions and a feedback loop that tells you what to do next.

01 · Context

Understand before prescribing

Who has the problem? What are they trying to do? What makes the problem worth solving now? I want those answers before jumping into solutions.

02 · Decision

Make trade-offs visible

Define the smallest useful version, what it deliberately does not include, and what evidence would change the decision.

03 · Build

Get something real in front of people

Use prototypes, workflows or working software to replace assumptions with something people can react to.

04 · Feedback

Let evidence decide the next move

Use usage, feedback and product signals to decide whether to iterate, change direction or stop.
